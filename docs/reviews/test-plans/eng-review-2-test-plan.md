## Affected Pages/Routes
- `/shift/` and its htmx partials (job list, My disputes, new-data notice, print)
- `/area/`, `/disputes/`, `/line/` operator drill-down partials

## Key Interactions to Verify
- A lead ticks 3 jobs, picks a reason, and submits; the supervisor confirms 2 and rejects 1 with notes; the lead's "My disputes" shows each status
- My Shift shows "New data from [time] · Refresh" after ingest, and the table changes only on click

## Edge Cases
- An open dispute form when new data arrives is untouched
  Value: protects=no lost dispute input on refresh; fails_when=poll swaps the table; why_new=new D5 behavior; seam=none; no_claim=browser-specific focus quirks
- The print sheet when the hub is stale reads "STALE: data stopped at …"
  Value: protects=honest paper; fails_when=header uses print time only; why_new=new D6 behavior; seam=none; no_claim=printer rendering
- A 3rd shift that crosses midnight opens as one window for its lead
  Value: protects=D21 default window on midnight shifts; fails_when=window split by calendar date; why_new=combines D21 and eng D2; seam=none; no_claim=none beyond protects

## Critical Paths
- Every operator-bearing URL (pages and htmx partials, print, queue) × every role × Gate 1 off/on renders identity only as policy allows
  Value: protects=no operator names or rates leak before Gate 1 or outside scope; fails_when=any view renders operator.name directly or skips the scope filter; why_new=new D2 policy; seam=none; no_claim=SSO group mapping in production
- Gate 1 letters match between a page and its partials, and differ across loads
  Value: protects=consistent, untrackable anonymization; fails_when=letters regenerate per request or persist across loads; why_new=new D3 behavior; seam=none; no_claim=re-identification by other means on tiny crews
- A multi-job dispute creates per-job rows; a per-job decision excludes exactly those jobs
  Value: protects=exact exclusions; fails_when=one decision applies to all jobs or exclusions miss a job; why_new=new D4 model; seam=none; no_claim=none beyond protects
